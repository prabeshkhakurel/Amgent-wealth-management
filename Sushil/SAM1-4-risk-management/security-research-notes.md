# Security Research Notes (SAM1-4)

Summary of research supporting the cybersecurity risk register.

## Zero Trust
Zero Trust assumes no user, device or connection is trusted by default. Access is continuously verified using identity, device compliance, risk signals and least-privilege permissions, rather than relying on network location alone.

## Microsoft Intune (BYOD)
Intune supports BYOD risk controls through device enrolment, compliance policies and app protection policies. It can require encryption, screen-lock and an approved device posture before allowing access to organisational resources, reducing risk from unmanaged personal devices.

## MFA and Microsoft Defender
Multi-factor authentication reduces the risk of unauthorised access when passwords are compromised. Microsoft Defender adds phishing, malware, endpoint and network protection, supporting detection, alerting and response for these risks.

## Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
Microsoft Purview DLP can identify sensitive information and apply controls to prevent unauthorised sharing through email, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive and endpoint devices, directly addressing the data leakage risk.
