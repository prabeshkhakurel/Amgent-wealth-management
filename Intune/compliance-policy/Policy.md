# Device Compliance Policy

## Objective

Configure an Intune compliance policy to ensure managed devices meet Amgent Wealth Management security requirements.

## Pre-Implementation

The existing Intune compliance policy environment was reviewed.

During the baseline assessment, existing compliance policies could not be retrieved from the portal.

## Planned Requirements

The compliance policy will assess approved security requirements including:

- Device encryption
- Secure Boot
- Antivirus/antimalware status
- Minimum operating system requirements

## Implementation

The compliance policy will be created after the required licensing and permissions are confirmed.

## Testing

The enrolled device will be evaluated against the compliance policy.

Expected result:

The approved device should report as compliant after meeting all required security controls.

Pending implementation.
