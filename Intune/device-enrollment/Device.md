# Device Enrollment

## Objective

Enroll the approved Amgent Wealth Management device into Microsoft Intune for centralized device management.

## Pre-Implementation

The Intune device inventory was reviewed before implementation.

No managed devices were displayed during the baseline review.

## Implementation

The approved device will be enrolled into Microsoft Intune after the required licensing, permissions, and device access are confirmed.

## Testing

After enrollment, the device will be checked in:

Microsoft Intune → Devices → All devices

Expected result:

The enrolled device should appear and report successfully to Microsoft Intune.
